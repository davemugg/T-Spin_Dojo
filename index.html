      Rules follow the modern guideline: 10×20 field with a 7-piece bag, Super Rotation System with wall kicks, hold, ghost piece, and lock delay with 15 move resets. A T-spin needs the T to lock right after a rotation with at least three of its four corners blocked. In build drills you drop one to three pieces first to create the setup, then the T. Any placement that keeps a T-spin double possible is accepted, and Hold lets you use the build pieces in any order. A double also needs two lines cleared. If only the back corners are blocked it counts as a mini, which does not satisfy these drills.
